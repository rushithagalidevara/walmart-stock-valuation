# Walmart Stock Valuation Analysis

Equity valuation of Walmart Inc. (NYSE: WMT), based on the Ivey Publishing case *"Valuing Wal-Mart — 2010"*, using a three-stage Dividend Discount Model (DDM), CAPM, and relative (P/E) valuation against peers.

## Snapshot: Walmart, FY2010 (ended Jan 31, 2010)

| Metric | FY2009 | FY2010 |
|---|---|---|
| Net sales | $401.1B | $405.0B |
| Net income | $13.4B | $14.3B |
| EPS (diluted) | $3.40 | $3.71 |
| Trailing P/E | — | 14.7x |
| Return on equity | — | 20.1% |

## Relative Valuation vs. Peers

| Metric | Walmart | Target | S&P 500 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trailing P/E | 14.7x | 15.6x | 18.0x |
| Projected P/E | 13.4x | 13.6x | 12.3x |
| PEG ratio | 1.41 | 1.23 | 1.66 |
| 2010 ROE | 20.1% | 16.2% | — |
| 2010 LT Debt/Equity | 0.51x | 0.99x | — |

Walmart traded at a discount to both Target and the broader market on trailing earnings, despite posting a higher return on equity and carrying roughly half Target's leverage — the kind of gap a DDM/relative-valuation exercise is built to test.

## Methodology

- **DDM:** A three-stage model (high-growth → transition → maturity) projecting EPS and dividends forward, discounted at a CAPM-derived required return, with a terminal value based on a long-run maturity growth rate.
- **CAPM:** Cost of equity estimated from the risk-free rate, market risk premium, and Walmart's beta.
- **Relative valuation:** Trailing and projected P/E and PEG ratios benchmarked against Target, the broader retail industry, and the S&P 500.
- **Data:** Walmart's FY2009–FY2010 income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement; 36 years of EPS/dividend history (1974–2010); daily WMT and S&P 500 price history; and peer P/E/PEG/ROE comparables — all sourced from the case's supporting data file.
